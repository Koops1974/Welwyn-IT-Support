Our Specialised Commercial Services

Delivered as individual projects or as a single, joined-up site technology plan.

1

Managed Ruckus Wireless Solutions

High-density, enterprise-grade Wi-Fi built for demanding environments.

Unreliable Wi-Fi leads to dropped calls, frustrated staff and lost productivity. We supply and deploy Ruckus wireless solutions, known for superior signal strength, beamforming technology and high-density performance.

  • Full site surveys: professional heatmapping to eliminate dead zones and optimise access point placement.
  • Turnkey supply & installation: professional cabling and seamless access point setup.
  • Fully managed service: proactive monitoring, guest Wi-Fi portal management, security patching and ongoing optimisation.

Ideal for: corporate offices, hospitality, educational facilities, warehouses and multi-tenant buildings.

2

Commercial CCTV Supply & Installation

Crystal-clear security and peace of mind for your premises.

Protect your site, staff and assets with modern IP CCTV technology. We design custom camera layouts to ensure total coverage without unnecessary redundancy.

  • HD & 4K vision: high-definition video recording with superior night vision capability.
  • Remote mobile access: secure real-time viewing and playback from your smartphone, tablet or desktop, anywhere.
  • Smart motion analytics: intelligent alerts for intrusion detection and line crossing.
  • Compliant & secure: local storage, secure network routing and setup assistance for regulatory compliance.
3

Paxton Access Control & Door Entry

Intelligent, reliable and scalable door security.

Control who enters your facility with industry-leading Paxton access control systems. From single standalone doors to centralised, multi-building commercial sites, we handle end-to-end supply, installation and ongoing technical support.

  • Flexible entry options: smart fobs, keycards, PIN codes or mobile smartphone entry.
  • Paxton Net2 & Paxton10 support: multi-door administration, time-based access permissions and automated visitor logging.
  • Video & audio intercoms: integrated door entry intercoms allowing secure verification before granting access.
  • System integration: seamless pairing with fire alarms, turnstiles and automated doors.
4

Digital Signage Solutions

Dynamic screen management to inform, direct and engage.

Replace static posters with modern digital displays that grab attention and streamline site communication.

  • Commercial hardware: high-brightness displays rated for 16/7 or 24/7 continuous operation.
  • Centralised content management: update announcements, marketing campaigns, directories or safety protocols from a simple web interface.
  • Tailored installations: reception screens, menu boards, corporate communications and interactive wayfinding touchscreens.

Commercial Projects: Common Questions

Practical answers before you book a site assessment.

Which areas do you cover?

Projects are based around Welwyn, Welwyn Garden City, Knebworth, Hatfield, Hertford and the surrounding Hertfordshire area. For larger roll-outs further afield, get in touch and we can discuss coverage.

Can you work with our existing network, cameras or door entry?

Yes. The starting point is an audit of what is already installed. Where equipment is sound it can often be integrated or retained, so you only replace what genuinely needs replacing.

Do you provide ongoing support after installation?

Yes. Managed Wi-Fi monitoring, maintenance visits and day-to-day technical support are available, along with ad-hoc help if you prefer to call things in as needed.

How does the free site assessment work?

We start with a short conversation about the site and what you need it to do, followed by an on-site survey where useful. You then receive a written project quotation with no obligation to proceed.

Can installation work be carried out outside business hours?

Where possible, yes. Evening or weekend work can be arranged to keep disruption to staff, customers and trading hours to a minimum.
